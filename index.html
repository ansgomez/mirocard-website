MiroCard

A brand-new product, bringing new digital services in a smart-card form factor.

Batteryless

MiroCard's optimized hardware needs very little light to operate, and it wakes up within few seconds.

Efficient

The MiroCard can sense its environment and communicate via BLE multiple times per second.

Secure

When your MiroCard is in your wallet, it cannot be remotely powered, keeping your data safely in pocket.

High-performance.
Card-Sized.

Compared to passive RFID cards, the active MiroCard has significantly more processing power and memory. This opens up new, secure applications with web-based authentication.

Universal Compatibilty. Enabled by Bluetooth.

MiroCards are compatible with millions of BLE compatible devices.

Technical Specifications

The MiroCard has been optimized for fast wake-up times, even in indoor lighting conditions: 250 lx - 2600 lx.

Environment Luminosity Wake-up time Input Power Performance
Natural + indoor light 2600 lx 1.7 seconds 980 uW 16 packet/s
Bright indoor light 1000 lx 4 seconds 370 uW 6 packet/s
Office light 500 lx 7.7 seconds 180 uW 3 packet/s
Overhead light 250 lx 16 seconds 85 uW ~1 packet/s

Open-Source

The MiroCard and MiroReader app are designed by Andres Gomez, inspired by the Transient BLE Node project developed at ETH Zurich. The Transient BLE Node project is an open-source project released under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License. The MiroCard hardware, firmware, and MiroReader app are open-source projects released under an MIT License.
